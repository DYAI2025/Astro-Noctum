# CON-dark-luxury-aesthetic: Dark OLED-First Luxury Aesthetic

**Category**: Business

**Status**: Active

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-product-owner](../stakeholders.md)

## Description

The visual design must follow a dark OLED-first approach with an obsidian/gold palette. The Obsidian Core renders in pure OLED black (`#010409`). Bioluminescent elements use gold (`#D4AF37`) and sapphire blue (`#3b82f6`). Wu-Xing element colors are used for elemental associations. The overall aesthetic must convey mathematical precision fused with spiritual depth.

## Rationale

The dark luxury aesthetic reinforces the product's positioning as a serious astrological tool, not a casual entertainment app. OLED black maximizes contrast for the bioluminescent Fusion Ring visualization and reduces battery consumption on mobile OLED screens. The obsidian/gold palette evokes the fusion of ancient wisdom and modern precision described in the True North document.

## Impact

- No light mode is provided; the entire UI assumes a dark background
- All color choices must be validated against OLED black for contrast and readability
- Component libraries and third-party UI elements must be themed or replaced to match the palette
- Accessibility (WCAG contrast ratios) must be verified against the dark background
- Wu-Xing element colors (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) must be defined as design tokens
