# US-weekly-overview: Weekly Life Area Overview

**Status**: Draft

**Source**: [GOAL-vibes-weekly-insights](../goals/GOAL-vibes-weekly-insights.md)

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-end-user](../stakeholders.md)

## User Story

As a user, I want an overview of my week across life areas so that I can orient myself on what matters.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All 7 life areas are present: Freundschaften, Liebe, Sex/Zärtlichkeit, Beruf, Alltag, Karriere, Gesundheit
- [ ] Each area has: 1 short statement + 1 tendency label (e.g., Spannung, Offenheit)
- [ ] Scannable within 10 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Content refreshes weekly (Monday or configurable)
