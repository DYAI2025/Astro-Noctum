# Architecture

## Purpose

This document describes the system architecture for Astro-Noctum. It defines the components, their boundaries, and how they interact to satisfy the approved goals and requirements (`1-spec/CLAUDE.spec.md`). The design principle is **simplest design that makes every approved requirement satisfiable** — complexity is introduced only where a simpler alternative fails to satisfy a requirement or violates a constraint.
